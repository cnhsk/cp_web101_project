About the Event

AI Builders Weekend is an intensive two-day experience designed specifically for domain experts who have big ideas but lack traditional coding skills.

In this hands-on workshop, you’ll learn to leverage AI tools to build and ship a functional software prototype from scratch.

We welcome builders from all backgrounds to join a supportive community, bridge the gap between concept and code, and turn your vision into reality.

What to Expect

Come with an Idea. Leave with an MVP

  • Learn AI-Driven Development: You don’t need to be a programmer to build software.

    We teach you how to leverage cutting-edge AI tools to transform your specific domain expertise into working code, guiding you step-by-step from initial concept to a functional prototype.

  • Ship Your Project: Stop waiting for the right moment.

    In this intensive, hands-on weekend, you will move beyond theory to build and deploy a real software solution to the internet, giving you a tangible product by the end of the second day.

  • Join a Collaborative Community: You aren't building alone.

    Connect with fellow domain experts, collaborate with teammates in small groups, and tap into ongoing support that continues well after the weekend concludes.

Event Schedule

Day 1: AI Foundations, Ecosystem & Core Stack

Goal: Understanding the landscape, mastering prompting, and establishing the dev environment.

Morning Session (10:00 – 12:00)

  • 10:00 – 10:45 - Sprint 1: The State of AI & Systems. Overview of current AI models, LLM capabilities, and how "Vibe Coding" is reshaping the dev landscape.
  • 10:45 – 11:00 - Break
  • 11:00 – 12:00 - Sprint 2: Advanced Prompting Techniques. Deep dive into the prompting frameworks (Role, Task, Context, Style, Goals) and creating effective system prompts.

Lunch (12:00 – 14:00)

Afternoon Session (14:00 – 16:00)

  • 14:00 – 14:45 - Sprint 3: Claude Code & IDE Setup. Configuring the local development environment, Claude Code Desktop app, and initial system checks.
  • 14:45 – 15:00 - Break
  • 15:00 – 15:45 - Sprint 4: Version Control Foundations. Integrating GitHub for version control and environment synchronization.
  • 15:45 – 16:00 - Break

Evening Session (16:00 – 19:00)

  • 16:00 – 16:45 - Sprint 5: Backend Architecture (Supabase). Setting up your database, authentication, and API layer.
  • 16:45 – 17:00 - Break
  • 17:00 – 17:45 - Sprint 6: Deployment Fundamentals (Vercel). Understanding the build/deploy cycle and setting up your Vercel pipeline.
  • 17:45 – 18:00 - Break
  • 18:00 – 18:45 - Sprint 7: Open Lab. Review of the day's setup, troubleshooting specific local issues, and roadmap planning for Day 2.
  • 18:45 – 19:00 - Closing of Day 1 & Networking.

Day 2: Build, Integrate & Launch

Goal: Building a functional MVP, polishing the product, and demoing to the community.

Morning Session (10:00 – 12:00)

  • 10:00 – 10:45 - Sprint 1: Project Scoping & Planning. Defining the brief, 3-feature rule, and technical mapping.
  • 10:45 – 11:00 - Break
  • 11:00 – 12:00 - Sprint 2: Frontend Scaffold. Using Claude Code to generate the UI shell and navigation.

Lunch (12:00 – 14:00)

Afternoon Session (14:00 – 16:00)

  • 14:00 – 14:45 - Sprint 3: Data & Auth Integration. Connecting the frontend to Supabase and enabling user sign-in.
  • 14:45 – 15:00 - Break
  • 15:00 – 15:45 - Sprint 4: AI Feature Integration. Adding LLM-powered features (e.g., auto-generation, search) to the product.
  • 15:45 – 16:00 - Break

Evening Session (16:00 – 19:00)

  • 16:00 – 16:45 - Sprint 5: Debugging & Quality Control. Automated testing, manual review, and fixing bugs with Claude Code.
  • 16:45 – 17:00 - Break
  • 17:00 – 17:45 - Sprint 6: Final Polish & Deploy. Launching to production on Vercel and finalizing the live URL.
  • 17:45 – 18:00 - Break
  • 18:00 – 18:45 - Sprint 7: Demo Day. Participant project showcases, peer feedback, and community reflection.
  • 18:45 – 19:00 - Closing of Day 2 & Networking.
